# Third-party attribution

## Chess piece drawings

The twelve chess piece SVG files in `dist/assets/` are generated from python-chess 1.11.2's SVG piece definitions, based on the Cburnett chess pieces by Colin M. L. Burnett. They are used under the GPL terms supplied with python-chess. Source definitions: https://github.com/niklasf/python-chess/blob/v1.11.2/chess/svg.py . The GPL license text is included as `LICENSE`.

## python-chess

python-chess, copyright Niklas Fiekas and contributors, GPL-3.0-or-later. Used offline for PGN parsing, legal move generation and SVG rendering. It is not loaded as a runtime website dependency. https://github.com/niklasf/python-chess

## Stockfish

Stockfish 19, GPL-3.0, was used offline for analysis. No engine binary is distributed with this site. Official source and releases: https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish . The analysis manifest identifies the engine release and search budgets.

## Game records

The case study uses chickenboi2's completed public games from Lichess. Original games are linked from the explorer and model lab. This is a frozen archive; it does not access private account data or send visitors' practice moves to a server.
